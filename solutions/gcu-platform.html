How It Works

The Room Runs Itself. The Software Watches.

This is the idea the whole platform is built on. Every control decision is made by the GCU in the room, not by a server somewhere else. GCU Manager and GCU Cloud configure the rules, record what happens and show it to your team. If the network drops, the room carries on exactly as programmed.

  • ✓ Schedules with sunrise and sunset ramps for lights
  • ✓ Sensor rules that hold temperature, humidity, CO2 and VPD in range
  • ✓ Grow programs that step through Clone, Veg and Flower on their own
  • ✓ Timed irrigation runs with an on-time guard on every output
  • ✓ Primary and backup sensors, so a drifting probe stands out
  • ✓ Local alarm flasher and sounder when a room needs a person
1

Sense

CO2, temperature, humidity, light and leaf wetness arrive over a wired RS-485 Modbus bus. No batteries, no wireless dropouts.

2

Decide

The GCU compares readings with the program for today and switches lights, fans, cooling, dehumidifiers, CO2 and valves.

3

Report

Readings and every state change stream to GCU Manager or GCU Cloud, where your team sees, adjusts and keeps records.

LWV-IO-4R2D Coming soon

GCU I/O MODULE
  • Four solid-state dry contacts for low-voltage control circuits, up to 60 V AC or DC and 2 A: contactor coils for fans, pumps and dehumidifiers, CO2 valves and equipment control inputs
  • A wiring check on every contact, so an open wire or a blown fuse shows up before the load is needed
  • Two 0-10 V outputs for dimming, valves or fan speed, sink or source, set in software, with the line read back
  • Comms watchdog with a safe state per channel if the bus goes quiet
  • 35 mm DIN-rail module with lever terminals, 24 VDC, firmware updates over RS-485
→ Switch it. Dim it. One DIN-rail module per room

MINI-SPLIT HVAC CONTROL
