The Challenge

Growing Is Hard. Systems Shouldn't Make It Harder.

Most indoor farms cobble together equipment from multiple vendors — different controllers, different software, nothing talks to anything else. Operators spend more time managing systems than growing crops.

  • ✗ Siloed systems that don't communicate
  • ✗ Manual monitoring across multiple screens
  • ✗ No historical data for crop optimization
  • ✗ Alarm fatigue from disconnected alerts
  • ✗ Vendor finger-pointing when things break
Our Solution

Unified Control, Single Source of Truth

We design and deploy integrated automation systems where everything works together — climate, irrigation, fertigation, lighting, and monitoring — all visible and controllable from one interface.

  • ✓ One dashboard for entire operation
  • ✓ Automated sequences and recipes
  • ✓ Historical trending and analytics
  • ✓ Smart alerting with context
  • ✓ One integrator who owns the outcome
