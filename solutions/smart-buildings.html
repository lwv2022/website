The Challenge

Legacy Lighting Wastes Energy and Lacks Visibility

Most commercial buildings still run on fixed schedules or manual switches. Lights stay on in empty rooms. There's no data on how spaces are actually used. Energy bills pile up while facility teams fly blind.

  • ✗ Lights on 24/7 in low-occupancy areas
  • ✗ No visibility into space utilization
  • ✗ Manual controls that nobody uses correctly
  • ✗ Expensive retrofits with complex wiring
Our Solution

Wireless Controls That Actually Work

We deploy enterprise-grade wireless lighting systems that install in hours, not weeks. Occupancy sensing, daylight harvesting, and scheduling happen automatically — while you get real-time analytics on every zone.

  • ✓ Wireless sensors — no new wiring needed
  • ✓ Occupancy & daylight-driven automation
  • ✓ Real-time dashboards and energy reporting
  • ✓ Integration with BMS, HVAC, and security
