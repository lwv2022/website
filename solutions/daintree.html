FAQ

Common Questions

Who owns Daintree now?

Daintree is now part of Current (formerly GE Current). The platform has evolved through Daintree Networks → GE → Current → Hubbell acquisition in 2022.

What's DLC certification?

Daintree Networked is DLC (DesignLights Consortium) certified, qualifying installations for utility rebates and incentive programs.

Can I retrofit existing fixtures?

Yes. The LCA accessory kit and WFA100 adapters enable any 0-10V fixture to join the Daintree network, regardless of manufacturer.
