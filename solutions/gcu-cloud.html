Why GCU Cloud

We Set Out to Build the Best Grow Control System There Is

We think we did. Here is what we built into it, and why each part matters when a crop is on the line.

The room never waits for the cloud

Every control decision is made by the GCU in the room. The schedules, the sensor rules and the grow program live on the controller, so a dropped connection changes nothing for your plants.

Every change is confirmed

Tap Off, On or Auto, or set a light level, and the GCU answers back when it has done it. You are never left wondering whether a command arrived.

It tells you why

Every output shows what is in charge of it right now: a schedule, a sensor rule, a manual hold or the switch on the panel. Every room carries a plain-language status with the reason behind it.

Readings you can trust

Each sensor read is marked good or not. A probe that goes silent is called out, never left showing its last number, and where a backup sensor is fitted it sits beside the primary so drift stands out.

The crop, not just the climate

Grow programs step through the stages on their own. Plantings, harvests and photos are recorded against each room, and AI reviews every room every night, with the evidence behind each finding.

One team behind all of it

We design the controller, write its firmware, run the cloud and build the app. When you need help, you reach the people who made it.

Every automation decision is made by the GCU on site. GCU Cloud sets up the rules and shows you what is happening. If the connection drops, the GCU carries on with the same schedules, rules and grow program, and the cloud picks up again when it reconnects.

AI Insights

An AI Review of Every Room, Every Night

Each night, AI reviews the day for every room: the readings, how hard each piece of equipment worked, which schedules and rules were in charge, what your team changed, the weather outside and your grow program's targets. It adds what it finds to your Insights page with the evidence behind each point, and a short weekly summary tells you what to look at first.

"The dehumidifier ran 40% longer this week to hold the same humidity. Worth checking the filter or coil."

"The exhaust fan runs during CO2 enrichment every morning, so most of the CO2 is vented."

"Temperature jumps 6°F in the 20 minutes after lights-on. A longer sunrise ramp would soften it."

Examples of the kind of finding it looks for. What it reports depends on your rooms.

AI suggests; it never acts

It does not change settings, schedules or equipment. Your GCU keeps making every control decision.

Only real numbers

Every finding is checked against your data before you see it. If it cannot be backed up, it is not shown.

Your data stays yours

Data sent for review is not used to train models. Prefer not to use it? We will switch it off for your site.

Good to Know

Common Questions

What do I need?
A GCU with an internet connection, by Wi-Fi or through your site router. We can supply a managed LTE gateway for sites without one. Anyone on your team can sign in from a web browser, and the LWV-GCU phone app is coming soon.
How are alerts sent?
By email, to the people you choose, per GCU.
Can I change settings at the GCU itself?
Yes. The LWV-GCU app (coming soon) connects to the GCU over Bluetooth when you are in the room, even with no signal, and GCU Cloud shows any changes made there.
Can I run it on site instead?
Yes. GCU Manager is the same platform on a server at your facility.
