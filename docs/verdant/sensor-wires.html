Push the sensor wires in

Three rooms have a new CO2 sensor the controller cannot hear. The box has power, the data line works, so the fix is almost certainly a sensor wire that is not gripped in its connector. No tools, about two minutes per room.

The whole job, in one line: open the grey box, find the row of five orange-lever connectors, lift each lever on 2, 3, 4 and 5, push the wire in until it stops, drop the lever, tug.

Inside the box

Two rows of connectors with orange levers. The sensor cable lands on the lower row of five, under the white controller module.

The five positions

Numbers are printed on the white tags. The wire colours are what you should see.

  • 2
    Red — 12 V power to the sensor
  • 3
    Black — ground
  • 4
    Data A — the sensor's A wire
  • 5
    Data B — the sensor's B wire

How the connector works

These connectors have no screws. The orange lever opens a spring clamp. A wire that is pushed in with the lever down only goes part way and may not be gripped at all.

Steps for each room

  1. Open the grey box

    Leave the power on. Find the lower row of five connectors, under the white controller module.

  2. Position 2 — red wire

    Lift the orange lever. Push the red wire in until it stops. Close the lever. Tug it.

  3. Position 3 — black wire

    Same again: lift, push, close, tug.

  4. Positions 4 and 5 — the two data wires

    Same again on both. Do not swap them with each other.

  5. Close the box and wait two minutes

    The sensor warms up, then its readings appear on the room's page in GCU Cloud. We can see it from our side too.

!
If a wire slides out when you tug it, its bare end is too short. Strip about 10 mm of insulation and push it in again with the lever up.
STOP
Do not move wires between positions. Red stays on 2, black on 3. If the data wires end up swapped the sensor stays silent.
