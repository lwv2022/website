IoT Cellular Services Agreement

This Agreement governs the provision of managed cellular connectivity services (cellular data only — no hardware) provided by Legacy West Ventures to the Customer identified at the time of sign-up.

Version 1.0 — Effective March 1, 2026 — Cellular Services Only. Hardware purchases are governed by a separate Sales Order.

📌 Key Terms at a Glance

  • Billing date: 1st of each month (invoiced in advance)
  • Payment method required: Credit card on file — auto-charged monthly
  • Grace period: 15 calendar days after the 1st before service suspension
  • Reactivation fee: $45.00 per line if suspended for non-payment
  • Minimum term: 12 months from service activation date
  • Cancellation notice: 30 days written notice required

1. Parties

Provider: Legacy West Ventures, LLC (“LWV” or “Provider”), a California limited liability company.
Customer: The entity or individual identified in the online enrollment form or executed Order Form (“Customer”).

This Agreement is entered into as of the date the Customer submits a signed Order Form or completes the online enrollment process, whichever occurs first.

2. Services

Provider will supply managed LTE cellular data connectivity (“Services”) to Customer’s pre-installed IoT gateways or modems on a per-line basis. Services are subject to the plan selected at enrollment.

Plan Monthly Rate (per line) Annual Rate (per line) Included Data Notes
Basic Connectivity $30.00 / mo $306.00 / yr (save $54) 2 GB LTE Throttled to 256 kbps after 2 GB
Static IP $40.00 / mo $408.00 / yr (save $72) 2 GB LTE Dedicated static IP for direct device access
Managed $45.00 / mo $459.00 / yr (save $81) 2 GB LTE Remote monitoring, offline alerts, up to 10 local devices; additional device fees apply

Annual rates reflect a 15% prepaid discount and are billed as a single charge. Taxes not included. Rates are per SIM/line.

2.1 One-Time Setup Fee

A non-refundable setup fee of $65.00 per line is charged at activation to cover SIM provisioning, secure network configuration, and initial testing by LWV. This fee is separate from recurring service charges.

2.2 Out-of-Plan Data

On the Basic, Static IP, and Managed plans, data consumption beyond the included 2 GB allotment per billing cycle will be throttled to 256 kbps for the remainder of that cycle. Provider does not currently offer pay-per-use overage billing; customers requiring consistent high-throughput access should contact LWV to discuss a custom plan.

2.3 Service Limits

Services are provided for lawful IoT machine-to-machine (M2M) data communications only. Services may not be used for voice calling, video streaming, peer-to-peer file sharing, or as a primary internet connection for human end-users (tethering). Violation of this restriction may result in immediate suspension without notice.

3. Billing & Payment

3.1 Billing Cycle

Services are billed monthly in advance. Invoices are generated and credit cards are charged on the 1st of each calendar month. For lines activated mid-month, the first invoice will include a prorated charge for the remainder of the activation month plus the full charge for the following month.

3.2 Credit Card Requirement

A valid credit or debit card must be on file at all times. Customer authorizes Provider to charge the card on file automatically on each billing date for all amounts due under this Agreement, including recurring service fees, setup fees, reactivation fees, and applicable taxes. Customer is responsible for keeping payment information current. Provider is not liable for service interruptions caused by an expired or declined card.

3.3 Failed Payments & Grace Period

⚠ Late Payment Policy

If a scheduled charge fails or remains unpaid, Provider will retry the card on file up to two (2) additional times over the following three (3) business days and will send email notification to the address on file. If payment is not successfully collected within 15 calendar days of the original due date (the “Grace Period”), Provider may suspend all affected lines without further notice.

3.4 Reactivation Fee

🚫 Reactivation After Suspension

If service is suspended due to non-payment, a $45.00 per line reactivation fee will be assessed in addition to all past-due balances before service is restored. Reactivation requests must be submitted to support@legacywestventures.com. SIM reactivation is typically completed within 1 business day following full payment.

3.5 Disputed Charges

Customer must notify Provider in writing of any billing dispute within 30 days of the invoice date. Disputes submitted after 30 days will not be eligible for credit. Undisputed amounts remain due on their original due date regardless of any dispute.

3.6 Taxes

All fees are exclusive of federal, state, and local taxes, regulatory fees, and surcharges. Applicable taxes will be itemized on each invoice and are the responsibility of Customer.

4. Term & Renewal

4.1 Initial Term

The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months from the activation date of the first line provisioned under this Agreement (“Initial Term”). Customer is obligated to pay all monthly fees for the full Initial Term even if lines are deactivated early.

4.2 Annual Prepay

Customers who choose annual prepay billing shall be billed for 12 months in advance at the discounted annual rate. Annual prepay amounts are non-refundable except as expressly set forth in Section 5.2.

4.3 Auto-Renewal

After the Initial Term, the Agreement will automatically renew on a month-to-month basis at the then-current monthly rate unless either party provides 30 days written notice of non-renewal. Provider will send a renewal notice at least 30 days before the Initial Term expires.

5. Cancellation & Termination

5.1 Customer Cancellation

Customer may cancel individual lines or the entire Agreement by providing 30 days written notice to support@legacywestventures.com. Cancellations received mid-cycle will take effect at the end of the current billing month. No partial-month credits are issued.

5.2 Early Termination During Initial Term

If Customer terminates any line before the expiration of the Initial Term, an early termination fee (“ETF”) equal to the remaining months on the Initial Term multiplied by the monthly rate for that line will be assessed. Annual prepay customers may apply any remaining prepaid balance as a credit toward ETF charges; no cash refunds will be issued.

5.3 Termination by Provider

Provider may terminate this Agreement or suspend Services immediately and without liability upon: (a) non-payment beyond the Grace Period; (b) Customer’s violation of the Acceptable Use provisions in Section 2.3; (c) illegal or fraudulent use of Services; or (d) instructions from a carrier or regulatory authority.

6. Service Level & Network

Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain cellular connectivity on behalf of Customer. Services depend on third-party carrier networks and are not guaranteed to be error-free or uninterrupted. Provider does not guarantee specific throughput, latency, or uptime percentages except where expressly stated in a separately executed SLA addendum.

Provider reserves the right to change carrier partners, SIM types, or network routing with 30 days notice, provided that service quality is not materially degraded.

7. Customer Responsibilities

  • Maintain compatible, functional hardware capable of accepting Provider-provisioned SIMs.
  • Keep billing contact information and payment method current in Provider’s portal.
  • Promptly notify Provider of SIM loss, theft, or unauthorized use at support@legacywestventures.com.
  • Use Services only for lawful M2M IoT applications as described in Section 2.3.
  • Cooperate with Provider during troubleshooting and provide reasonable access to equipment when required.

8. Limitation of Liability

TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, PROVIDER’S TOTAL LIABILITY TO CUSTOMER FOR ANY CLAIMS ARISING UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE THREE (3) MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING THE CLAIM. IN NO EVENT SHALL PROVIDER BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST REVENUE OR DATA LOSS, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

9. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to hold the other party’s Confidential Information in confidence and not to disclose it to third parties without prior written consent. “Confidential Information” includes pricing, technical specifications, network configurations, and business terms under this Agreement. This obligation survives termination of the Agreement for two (2) years.

10. Governing Law & Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Any dispute that cannot be resolved through good-faith negotiation shall be submitted to binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association in Santa Clara County, California. The prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys’ fees.

11. Modifications

Provider reserves the right to modify these terms, pricing, or service descriptions with 30 days written notice via email to the address on file. Customer’s continued use of Services after the effective date of a change constitutes acceptance of the modified terms. If Customer objects to a material change in pricing, Customer may terminate without ETF by providing written notice within 15 days of the change notice.

12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the Order Form and any executed addenda, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an Order Form, the Order Form controls for commercial terms (pricing, term length, line quantities) and this Agreement controls for all other matters.
