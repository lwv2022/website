Arise · Replacing Growlink

Arise Fertigation Controller

The flow meter, the 7 Etatron pumps and the pipes stay as they are. The Growlink box comes out and an LWV controller takes its spot on the same wires, one in each building. It runs the dosing on its own, and the GCU Manager sets the recipes and logs every feed.

How it connects

Growlink box → our controllerSame spot, same wires. The flow meter's wire and the pump wires move across.
New I/O moduleCounts the flow meter's pulses, and turns the controller's 7 pump speeds into the milliamp signal the Etatrons already take.
Working EC + pH sensorsThe controller can check the mix and correct it again. Hand testing becomes a spot check.
Linked to the ManagerRecipes, feed logs and alerts sit with the irrigation, harvests and photos it already has.

What the controller does every second

Example numbers for pump 1. The real recipe and pump sizes come from the walk-through.

1 · Count3,643 ticks from the flow meter in the last minute30 gal/min3,643 ÷ 121.42 ticks per gallon
2 · RecipePump 1 is set to 2.0 ml per gallon60 ml/min30 gal/min × 2.0 ml/gal
3 · SpeedA 10 L/h pump does 167 ml/min flat out36% speed60 ÷ 167
4 · Signal4 mA is stopped, 20 mA is full speed9.8 mA4 + 36% × 16 mA, sent to pump 1

Then the same for pumps 2 to 7. If the flow rises to 40 gal/min, every pump speeds up by the same share, so each gallon keeps the same mix.

No flow, no dosingNo ticks from the meter sends all 7 pumps to 4 mA (stopped).
A ceiling per pumpEach pump has a top speed it never goes past, whatever the recipe or a correction says.
Small corrections onlyEC and pH can nudge the recipe in small capped steps, and only while the sensors read sensible values.
Runs without the PiIf the Manager or the network drops, the controller keeps feeding on the last recipe and logs locally.

Who does what

Fertigation controller

Runs the dosing by itself, second by second
  • Reads the flow meter's count every second
  • Sets the speed of each of the 7 pumps
  • Reads the EC and pH sensors and makes small corrections
  • Applies the safety rules above
  • Keeps its own log if the network is down

GCU Manager

Sets it up and keeps watch
  • Recipes: ml per gallon for each pump, for each stage
  • Which recipe is running, tied to the feed schedule it already runs
  • A log of every feed: gallons, ml from each pump, EC, pH
  • Alerts: EC or pH out of range, no flow while a valve is open, a pump alarm
  • Feed history in the harvest report card and the AI review

Switching over without risk

  1. Walk-through. Photos of the wall, Growlink's settings for each pump, and what each pump doses.
  2. Bench. Build the first controller and run it against one pump before anything goes on site.
  3. Listen only. Fit it next to Growlink in the first building, reading the flow meter, and check our gallon count matches Growlink's.
  4. One pump. Move pump 1 to our controller and check it against Mitch's hand samples, then move the other six.
  5. Sensors. Fit the EC and pH sensors and turn the corrections on last.
  6. Second building. A copy of the first controller, through the same steps.
  7. Cancel Growlink once both buildings have run clean for an agreed time.

To find out on the walk-through

  • Does each building have its own mix room, flow meter and 7 pumps? The plan assumes it does.
  • What each pump doses (nutrient parts, pH up or down) and its size in litres an hour.
  • Growlink's current setting for each pump.
  • Is Growlink wired straight to the pumps, and does it switch anything else, such as a main feed pump?
  • Does the feed water go through the same pod valves the Manager already runs?
  • Which EC and pH meters were there, and why they stopped working.
