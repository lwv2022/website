Installing the GCU Manager

Arise · north wing · about 20 minutes
You need: the GCU Manager box, the black power injector, 2 network cables, a wall outlet

What this is: the GCU Manager is the small computer that collects data from all 76 pods and runs the website you log into. It needs one network cable and nothing else — power travels down that same cable. There is nothing to set up and no password to enter. Plug it in, mount it high, and text Tony.

1

Find the right router

Go to the north wing — the side without the restrooms. To be sure you're at the right router, check Wi-Fi on your phone: the strongest network there should be DemeGrow-NW. If you see DemeGrow-SW, you're in the wrong wing.

2

Mount the box as high as you can

High on a wall or post, antennas pointing up, clear lid facing out so you can see the lights. The higher it goes the better its Wi-Fi reaches the south wing. Not inside a metal cabinet and not behind metal — that blocks the signal.

3

Cable 1: router ➜ injector

One cable from any port marked LAN on the router — LAN1, LAN2 or LAN3, all three are empty — into the injector port marked DATA IN. Do not use the port marked WAN.

4

Cable 2: injector ➜ GCU Manager

The GCU Manager already has a blue cable coming out of the bottom — that is its only connection. Plug it into the injector port marked DATA & POWER OUT. Power and data both travel down this one cable.

5

Plug the injector into the wall

Last. The injector needs its own wall outlet — that is the only mains plug in the whole job. Within about a minute: POWER steady and ACT blinking.

6

Check, then text Tony

Look through the clear lid — you should see small lights on inside the box. Then text Tony that it's plugged in. He checks the rest from his end; you don't need to wait around.

Tip — test before you climb. If it's easy to do, connect everything on the ground first and check that the injector lights come on and the box lights up. Then take it up and mount it. Saves a second trip up the ladder.

What it looks like

Photos, what to check, and what to do if something is off
The GCU Manager. Antennas on top — mount so they point up. The blue network cable leaves the bottom. Do not open the lid — it is sealed against damp and there is nothing inside for you to touch.
The power injector. Two network ports, labelled on the case. DATA IN ➜ from the router. DATA & POWER OUT ➜ to the GCU Manager. The mains cord is on the far end. The two lights are POWER and ACT.

When you're done, this is what you should see

WhereWhat's right
Injector — POWEROn and steady. If it's off, the mains plug isn't in or the outlet is dead.
Injector — ACTBlinking. Blinking means data is moving. If it never lights, re-seat both network cables and check you used a LAN port on the router.
Through the box lidSmall lights on inside. They may flicker — that's normal.
The box itselfMounted high, antennas up, lid shut and latched, cable running down out of the way.

If something isn't right

What you seeWhat to do
No lights anywhereCheck the injector's mains plug and the outlet. The injector is the only thing that plugs into power.
POWER on, nothing in the boxAlmost always the two network cables are swapped. The router must go to DATA IN and the GCU Manager to DATA & POWER OUT.
The blue cable won't reachDon't stretch it. Use a network coupler and a second cable — any length up to about 300 ft is fine.
Not sure it's the right routerCheck Wi-Fi on your phone. At the right one, DemeGrow-NW is the strong network. DemeGrow-SW means the wrong wing.
Anything elseLeave it plugged in and text Tony. He can see the unit remotely and will tell you if anything needs moving.
Please don't: open the GCU Manager lid · unplug any pod to free up a cable · move or unplug anything else on the router · put the box in a metal cabinet or behind metal.
Why mount it high? The GCU Manager is wired to the north router, so the north pods reach it straight away. For the south wing it has to get there over Wi-Fi — so the higher and more in the open it sits, the better that works. If it turns out it can't hear the south side, that's fine and nothing is broken; Tony will sort it from his end.
