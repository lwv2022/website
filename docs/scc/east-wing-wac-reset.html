Restore offline lighting controllers on Floors 10 and 7 North

Prepared by Legacy West Ventures · September 30, 2026 · Time on site: about 30 minutes · Tools: flashlight, phone

Offline
EW.10.A.N

Floor 10 North lighting controller (WAC)

Also offline
EW.07.A.N

Floor 7 North lighting controller (WAC)

What you are doing You'll power-cycle the network cable that feeds each floor's lighting controllers. Unplug it, wait, and plug it back in. Nothing gets configured, and no tools or passwords are needed.
Call before you start Call Legacy West Ventures before you unplug anything. We'll watch the controllers live and tell you the moment each one is back online.

1. Where to go

Go to East Wing, Floor 8, Telecom Room 08.02. Find the PoE network switch for the lighting controls. That's the switch whose cables run to the upper floors (6 through 11). Its cables are labeled with the floor they feed.

Equipment may look different from older photos The switches in this room were replaced, so the model and port layout may not match earlier records. Go by the cable labels, not the port position.

2. Find the right cables by their labels

Each floor is fed by one cable from the switch, and that cable powers both of the floor's lighting controllers (North and South). Read the labels on the cables plugged into the switch and find these two:

Last recorded positions (January 2026, may have changed): Floor 10 on port G3, Floor 7 on port G6, modem on G1. Use these only as a hint for where to start looking.

If you can't find a clear label, stop and call us Don't unplug cables to find the right one by trial and error. Never unplug the modem or uplink cable, because that takes the whole East Wing lighting system offline. Before you pull a cable, write down which port it's in so it goes back in the same port.

3. Power-cycle the Floor 10 cable

  1. Find the cable labeled Floor 10Note which port it's in and whether that port's light is on.
  2. Unplug the cablePress the clip and pull the cable straight out of the switch.
  3. Wait 15 secondsThis lets the equipment on Floor 10 lose power completely.
  4. Plug it back into the same portPush until it clicks. Tug gently to make sure it's locked in. The port light should come on within a few seconds.
  5. Wait 5 minutesBoth Floor 10 controllers restart, so Floor 10 South drops offline briefly too. That's normal. We'll confirm by phone when Floor 10 North is back.

4. Power-cycle the Floor 7 cable

  1. Find the cable labeled Floor 7Note which port it's in.
  2. Unplug, wait 15 seconds, plug back inSame as for Floor 10. Put it back in the same port and push until it clicks.
  3. Wait 5 minutesFloor 7 South drops offline briefly too. We'll confirm when Floor 7 North is back.
If we confirm both are back, you're done Please take a photo of the switch with its cable labels readable and send it to us. It will update our records for the new equipment.

5. If a controller is still offline

The South controller on each floor is working, so the switch port is fine. The problem is between the floor's PoE splitter and the North controller.

  1. Go to that floor (10 or 7) and find the PoE splitterIt's a small box with one cable in and two cables out, usually labeled A and B. Look near the network drops, in the floor's telecom closet, or above the ceiling near the lighting controllers. Output A feeds North and B feeds South.
  2. Reseat output AUnplug the A cable, wait 10 seconds, and plug it back in firmly. Leave B alone, because it feeds the South controller, which is working.
  3. Check the North controller's lightFind the North controller (a small white box mounted at ceiling height on the North side).
    • Light off: it isn't getting power. Reseat the cable at the controller end. If it's still dark, call us. We may have you swap A and B briefly to test the splitter.
    • Light on, still offline: unplug the controller's cable for 10 seconds, plug it back in, and call us.
